The objective

The long way
up is the point.

Kilimanjaro is not technical climbing, but it is a serious high-altitude objective. The Machame, Lemosho & Marangu Routes gives us the time and terrain to let the body adapt: rainforest, volcanic moorland, wide alpine desert, and finally the summit slopes above the clouds.

Outland matches you with a vetted Tanzanian team and stays involved from your first training question through the final logistics check. You will carry your day kit while the local crew manages camp systems, meals, and the mountain’s practical rhythm.

Our focus is simple: a route that respects the mountain, a team that knows it intimately, and a plan you understand before you land in Tanzania.

Five ecosystems

Deliberate acclimatization

More time at the right elevations, daily check-ins, and a pace built for the objective.

Local leadership

The direct southern circuit

Machame 6-Day

6 days on trailMachame Gate · 1,800 mFastest itinerary

A compact Machame itinerary for climbers with a strong hiking base and limited time. It keeps the essential high-and-low acclimatization day at Lava Tower, then makes a longer push from Barranco directly to Barafu.

What makes it different
Compact pacing and longer transition days.
Summit profile
All options reach Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m / 19,341 ft after a pre-dawn ascent.
What is included
Airport pickup and drop-off, pre- and post-climb lodging as confirmed in your proposal, shared camp equipment, mountain meals, park and rescue fees, oxygen, local guide and porter team, and applicable taxes.
Day 1

Machame Gate to Machame Camp
Transfer from Arusha or Moshi to Machame Gate for registration, then climb through rainforest of fig trees, ferns, vines, birds, and possible monkey sightings to Machame Camp at 3,000 m.

Day 2

Machame Camp to Shira 2 Camp
Leave the forest for heather and moorland, crossing ridges and valleys with views toward Kibo and the Shira Plateau. Sleep at Shira 2 Camp, 3,840 m.

Day 3

Shira 2 to Lava Tower to Barranco
Climb to Lava Tower, 4,630 m, for lunch before descending to Barranco Camp, 3,960 m. This climb-high, sleep-lower day is central to acclimatization.

Day 4

Barranco Camp to Barafu Camp
Scramble the non-technical Barranco Wall, then continue through alpine desert directly to Barafu Camp, 4,640 m. This itinerary does not overnight at Karanga, so the day is longer; rest, eat early, and prepare for summit night.

Day 5

Barafu to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp
Begin around midnight for the steep climb to Stella Point, 5,739 m, and Uhuru Peak, 5,895 m. Descend via Barafu for a short rest, then continue to Mweka Camp, 3,100 m.

Day 6

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate
Descend through rainforest to Mweka Gate, 1,640 m, receive your summit certificate, and transfer to your hotel.

Travel bookends: arrive before the climb for airport pickup, briefing, gear check, dinner, and hotel rest; after the mountain, transfer back to your hotel before your planned departure. Exact hotels and transfers are confirmed in writing.

Machame 7-Day

7 days on trailMachame Gate · 1,800 mStrong hiking base with more acclimatization time

The same varied Machame approach with an extra overnight at Karanga. Splitting the move to Barafu creates a more deliberate rhythm before summit night.

What makes it different
A more measured approach with Karanga and Barafu separated.
Summit profile
All options reach Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m / 19,341 ft after a pre-dawn ascent.
What is included
Airport pickup and drop-off, pre- and post-climb lodging as confirmed in your proposal, shared camp equipment, mountain meals, park and rescue fees, oxygen, local guide and porter team, and applicable taxes.
Day 1

Machame Gate to Machame Camp
Register at Machame Gate, then hike 5–7 hours through rainforest alive with birds and monkeys to Machame Camp at 3,000 m.

Day 2

Machame Camp to Shira Camp
Leave the forest for moorland, heather, and volcanic rock. Climb 4–6 hours to Shira Camp, 3,840 m, with broad views of Kibo and the Shira Plateau.

Day 3

Shira Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco
Ascend to Lava Tower, 4,630 m, for lunch and acclimatization, then descend to Barranco Camp, 3,960 m. Expect 6–7 hours on the move.

Day 4

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp
Make the adventurous but non-technical Barranco Wall scramble, then cross ridges and valleys to Karanga Camp, 4,035 m. The shorter 4–5 hour day creates another acclimatization pause.

Day 5

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp
Move 3–4 hours through dry, rocky alpine desert to Barafu Camp, 4,640 m. Use the afternoon for rest, hydration, a hot meal, and summit preparation.

Day 6

Barafu to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp
Begin at midnight, climb toward Stella Point, 5,739 m, and continue to Uhuru Peak, 5,895 m. Descend to Barafu for rest, then continue to Mweka Camp, 3,100 m; allow 11–14 hours.

Day 7

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate
Descend 3–4 hours through rainforest to Mweka Gate, collect your summit certificate, and transfer to the hotel.

Travel bookends: arrive before the climb for airport pickup, briefing, gear check, dinner, and hotel rest; after the mountain, transfer back to your hotel before your planned departure. Exact hotels and transfers are confirmed in writing.

Lemosho 6-Day

6 days on trailLondorossi Gate · 2,100 mStrong hikers who want Lemosho scenery

A compact way to experience the quieter western forest and Shira Plateau. It combines Karanga and Barafu into one longer day, so route readiness and efficient daily habits matter.

What makes it different
Compact pacing and longer transition days.
Summit profile
All options reach Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m / 19,341 ft after a pre-dawn ascent.
What is included
Airport pickup and drop-off, pre- and post-climb lodging as confirmed in your proposal, shared camp equipment, mountain meals, park and rescue fees, oxygen, local guide and porter team, and applicable taxes.
Day 1

Londorossi Gate to Big Tree Camp
Drive to Londorossi Gate, 2,100 m, for registration and continue to the Lemosho trailhead. Trek 3–4 hours through rainforest, with possible monkeys and birdlife, to Big Tree Camp (Mti Mkubwa), 2,780 m.

Day 2

Big Tree Camp to Shira 2 Camp
Leave rainforest for rolling moorland and open views across the Shira Plateau. Trek 5–6 hours to Shira 2 Camp, 3,850 m.

Day 3

Shira 2 to Lava Tower to Barranco
Cross the plateau and climb to Lava Tower, 4,600 m, for lunch. Descend into Barranco Valley to sleep at Barranco Camp, 3,950 m, after roughly 7–8 hours.

Day 4

Barranco to Karanga to Barafu
Climb the non-technical Barranco Wall, continue to Karanga Camp, 4,050 m, for lunch, then move onward to Barafu Camp, 4,680 m. This is a demanding 7–8 hour day before the summit attempt.

Day 5

Barafu to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp
Set out around midnight for Stella Point, 5,739 m, and Uhuru Peak, 5,895 m. After summit time, descend to Barafu for a break and then to Mweka Camp, 3,100 m. Allow 11–14 hours.

Day 6

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate
Make the 3–4 hour rainforest descent to Mweka Gate, 1,640 m, collect your certificate, and transfer to the hotel.

Travel bookends: arrive before the climb for airport pickup, briefing, gear check, dinner, and hotel rest; after the mountain, transfer back to your hotel before your planned departure. Exact hotels and transfers are confirmed in writing.

Lemosho 7-Day

7 days on trailLondorossi Gate · 2,100 mMost balanced Lemosho option

The longer Lemosho option separates Karanga and Barafu into two days, giving the body more time to settle into altitude after the western approach and southern circuit.

What makes it different
A more measured approach with Karanga and Barafu separated.
Summit profile
All options reach Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m / 19,341 ft after a pre-dawn ascent.
What is included
Airport pickup and drop-off, pre- and post-climb lodging as confirmed in your proposal, shared camp equipment, mountain meals, park and rescue fees, oxygen, local guide and porter team, and applicable taxes.
Day 1

Londorossi Gate to Big Tree Camp
Register at Londorossi Gate, 2,100 m, then begin through rainforest toward Big Tree Camp (Mti Mkubwa), 2,780 m. Expect 3–4 hours of hiking.

Day 2

Big Tree Camp to Shira 2 Camp
Rise through moorland, rolling hills, volcanic terrain, lobelias, and groundsels to Shira 2 Camp, 3,850 m. Allow 5–6 hours.

Day 3

Shira 2 to Lava Tower to Barranco
Climb steadily to Lava Tower, 4,600 m, for lunch, then descend to Barranco Camp, 3,950 m. The 6–7 hour day pairs elevation gain with a lower sleeping altitude.

Day 4

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp
Start with the Barranco Wall scramble, then travel through ridges and valleys to Karanga Camp, 4,050 m. This 4–5 hour day is an important acclimatization stop.

Day 5

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp
Climb 3–4 hours through high alpine terrain to Barafu Camp, 4,680 m. Rest, hydrate, and prepare for the midnight summit departure.

Day 6

Barafu to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp
Climb overnight to Stella Point, 5,739 m, then along the crater rim to Uhuru Peak, 5,895 m. Descend via Barafu to Mweka Camp, 3,100 m, completing 11–14 hours of trekking.

Day 7

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate
Descend through dense rainforest to Mweka Gate, 1,640 m, receive your certificate, and transfer to your hotel in Moshi or Arusha.

Travel bookends: arrive before the climb for airport pickup, briefing, gear check, dinner, and hotel rest; after the mountain, transfer back to your hotel before your planned departure. Exact hotels and transfers are confirmed in writing.

The eastern hut approach

Marangu 5-Day

5 days on trailMarangu Gate · 1,860 mCompact altitude schedule

Follow the eastern approach through rainforest, moorland, and alpine desert, sleeping in shared mountain huts rather than tents. Five days leaves little acclimatization margin; discuss previous altitude experience and readiness with us before choosing this option.

What makes it different
Shared hut accommodation; ascent and descent follow the Marangu corridor. No dedicated acclimatization day.
Summit profile
All options reach Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m / 19,341 ft after a pre-dawn ascent.
What is included
Park, hut and rescue fees, mountain meals, guide and porter team, taxes, and mountain-gate transfers. Airport transfers and pre/post-climb hotel arrangements are confirmed in your written proposal.
Day 1

Marangu Gate to Mandara Huts
Transfer from Arusha to Marangu Gate (1,860 m) for registration. Walk through rainforest with birds and possible monkey sightings to Mandara Huts (2,700 m). Allow 4–5 hours; settle in, eat, and prepare for the next day.

Day 2

Mandara Huts to Horombo Huts
Climb from rainforest into open moorland with increasingly broad views toward Mawenzi. Reach Horombo Huts (3,720 m), then spend the afternoon resting, hydrating, and acclimatizing before dinner and overnight.

Day 3

Horombo Huts to Kibo Huts
Cross the exposed alpine desert and saddle toward Kibo Huts (4,700 m). Allow 5–6 hours. Eat early, organize warm layers and your headlamp, and rest before the overnight summit attempt.

Day 4

Kibo Huts to Uhuru Peak to Horombo Huts
Depart around midnight for the steep ascent to the crater rim via Gilman’s Point and onward to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m). Return down the same approach to Horombo Huts (3,720 m). This is the longest day: roughly 12–14 hours, subject to pace and conditions.

Day 5

Horombo Huts to Marangu Gate
Descend through moorland and rainforest to Marangu Gate (1,860 m), collect your certificate, and transfer to your hotel in Arusha for a shower, rest, and your final evening.

Travel bookends: arrive before the climb for airport pickup, briefing, gear check, dinner, and hotel rest; after the mountain, transfer back to your hotel before your planned departure. Exact hotels and transfers are confirmed in writing.

Training

Train for long
days, not a photo.

You do not need technical climbing experience for Kilimanjaro, but you should be ready for several consecutive days of sustained hiking and a long summit push at altitude. Start early: aerobic base, hills or stairs, loaded carries, lower-body strength, mobility, and recovery.

We will talk through your current routine and give practical recommendations. The goal is to arrive durable, calm, and able to enjoy the mountain—not simply survive the summit day.

Talk training with us
Your route matters. Marangu 5-Day and 6-Day use shared huts at Mandara, Horombo, and Kibo. Bring your personal sleeping bag, layers, boots, headlamp, and daily essentials; a hut is not a heated hotel. Machame and Lemosho use tented camps. Confirm the route-specific gear manifest with us.

Kilimanjaro FAQs

Your Kilimanjaro
questions, answered.

Do I need mountaineering experience?
Kilimanjaro is a high-altitude trek rather than a technical alpine climb. A strong hiking base, purposeful training, and respect for altitude matter more than rope-climbing experience.
Which Kilimanjaro option is right for me?
Compare Machame 6/7-Day, Lemosho 6/7-Day, and Marangu 5/6-Day. Marangu uses shared huts; its six-day option adds an acclimatization day at Horombo. The five-day option has the least acclimatization time. Machame 6-Day and Lemosho 6-Day are more compact; their seven-day counterparts separate Karanga and Barafu for a more measured rhythm.
Do I need travel insurance?
Comprehensive travel insurance, including emergency medical and evacuation coverage appropriate for high altitude, is required for this trip. Review the policy’s coverage, exclusions, and eligibility carefully.
What mountain equipment is provided?
The local operator provides shared mountain infrastructure, including airport pickup and drop-off, pre- and post-climb lodging as confirmed in your proposal, shared camp equipment, mountain meals, park and rescue fees, oxygen, local guide and porter team, and applicable taxes. Personal clothing, boots, sleeping bag, daypack, and individual essentials remain your responsibility unless confirmed otherwise in writing.
Should I get a medical checkup?
Yes. Speak with a qualified clinician before high-altitude travel, particularly if you have a health condition or use prescription medication.
What vaccines or medications do I need?
This is a conversation for a qualified travel-medicine clinician. Vaccines, malaria prevention, and any prescription medication depend on your health history, itinerary, and current guidance. Review current CDC Tanzania travel guidance →
Can I add a Tanzania safari?
Yes. We can build a Tanzania wildlife safari after your climb with recovery time and travel buffers that keep the overall trip realistic.
