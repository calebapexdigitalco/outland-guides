Mount Kilimanjaro

A system for
high altitude.

Kilimanjaro is a layering and weather-management objective. Your individual clothing and sleep system should be reliable from wet forest through a cold summit morning.

Before you purchase

Personal kit

Packed by you. Reviewed with us.

Technical outerwear

  • • Waterproof breathable hooded jacket
  • • Insulated down or synthetic jacket
  • • Fleece or soft-shell jacket
  • • Waterproof pants and fleece pants
  • • Optional rain poncho in wet season

Trekking layers

  • • 2 long-sleeve moisture-wicking shirts
  • • 1 short-sleeve shirt
  • • 2 hiking pants; optional shorts
  • • Long underwear and underwear
  • • Sports bras as needed

Feet, head & hands

  • • Waterproof, broken-in hiking boots + spare laces
  • • Thick wool/synthetic socks; optional liners
  • • Brimmed hat, knit hat, warm gloves
  • • Optional gaiters, balaclava, and camp shoes

Bags & equipment

  • • Four-season sleeping bag; optional liner
  • • Duffel for porter-carried gear; daypack
  • • Trekking poles and waterproof pack cover
  • • Headlamp plus spare batteries and dry bags

Hydration & accessories

Nalgene-style bottles, a water bladder, sunglasses or goggles, sun protection, lip balm, quick-dry towel, camera, and high-energy personal snacks. Keep your daily essentials easy to access.

Health & documents

Personal first-aid items, prescriptions, insect repellent, hand sanitizer, toilet paper, wet wipes, passport, visa, immunization record, insurance documents, and your trip receipt.

Shared mountain equipment

The operator provides shared mountain equipment: client and crew tents, mess and kitchen tents, dining tables and chairs, cooking/eating gear, foam mats, oxygen cylinders, and pulse oximeters for daily checks.

Volcán de Fuego

Light enough
to move well.

Fuego is a steep, exposed volcanic trek. Expect a warm approach, cooler wind at camp, dust underfoot, and a cold early-morning viewpoint. Keep your kit compact and built around layers.

Movement system

Broken-in hiking boots with traction, socks you trust, trekking poles, 20–30L daypack, and a rain cover. Volcanic grit and steep terrain reward simple, durable choices.

Layering system

Moisture-wicking shirt, fleece or active insulation, waterproof shell, hiking pants, beanie, gloves, and a warm puffy for camp and sunrise. Pack every layer even if the trailhead is warm.

Sun, ash & hydration

Bring at least two liters of water capacity, electrolytes, sunscreen, lip balm, sunglasses, and a buff or face covering for dust and wind. Carry snacks that work when cold.

Overnight essentials

Headlamp with spare batteries, personal medications, travel documents, sleeping bag appropriate for camp temperatures, and a small dry bag for electronics. Final overnight equipment depends on the confirmed route and camp setup.

Fuego packing principle

Carry what makes you warm, dry, hydrated, and sure-footed—not a huge pack. We will adjust this checklist to the forecast and your chosen itinerary before departure.

Tanzania Wildlife Safari

Comfort that
keeps you outside.

A safari is not a fashion shoot. Bring sun-ready layers, a small daily kit, and enough flexibility for cool morning drives, warm afternoons, dust, and lodge evenings.

Clothing

Lightweight trekking pants or shorts, breathable shirts, a long-sleeve layer, fleece or light insulated jacket for dawn, rain shell, and comfortable evening clothes. Choose neutral, practical colors.

In the vehicle

Binoculars if you have them, camera and spare memory/battery, reusable water bottle, sun hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, charging cable, and a small daypack for what stays close.

Sun & weather

Sun exposure is real, even on cool mornings. Pack sun protection, lip balm, insect repellent, and a light rain layer. A buff is useful for dust on open roads.

Personal essentials

Passport, visas as required, insurance, personal medications, a basic first-aid kit, and any travel-health supplies recommended by your clinician. Keep critical items in your daypack.

Pairing it with Kilimanjaro?

Keep a separate, easy-to-reach safari bag with clean layers, cameras, travel documents, and lodge clothing. You will appreciate the reset after the mountain.
