We will never pretend every mountain, climber, or season is the same. These are solid starting answers; a personal consult turns them into a real plan.

Ask us directly
What is your cancellation policy?

Plans can change. Our policy is designed to be clear and consistent while recognizing the advance planning, permits, and local operating costs behind an expedition.

Trip cancellation insurance is strongly recommended for every Outland Guides program. Government and operator permits can create meaningful non-recoverable costs, so our refund terms are firm.

Payment schedule

  • 50% is due to reserve your place.
  • The standard balance deadline is 60 days before departure. Closer-in bookings have a payment deadline confirmed in writing before you pay.
  • Any non-refundable registration fee must be itemized before payment; no undisclosed fee is deducted.
  • Cancel 120 or more days before the program start date: full refund less the registration fee.
  • Cancel 90-119 days before the program start date: 50% refund less the registration fee.
  • Cancel 89 or fewer days before the program start date: no refund.
  • Send every cancellation or refund request to our office by email within the applicable deadline.
  • If Outland Guides cancels or reschedules before the trip begins, all paid fees are refundable and/or transferable.

Your written trip agreement confirms payment timing and any disclosed registration fee or trip-specific terms before you commit. Read the full cancellation and booking policy →

How do I choose a good tour operator?
Look for a clear safety process, transparent inclusions, guide credentials, realistic summit language, and a team willing to discuss your experience before taking payment. Outland vets local operators, stays involved through preparation and logistics, and helps match the route to you—not the other way around.
How much does an expedition cost?
Kilimanjaro pricing is confirmed around one of six options: Machame 6-Day, Machame 7-Day, Lemosho 6-Day, Lemosho 7-Day, Marangu 5-Day, or Marangu 6-Day. Your proposal reflects route length, group size, dates, park costs, and selected lodging and transfers. International flights, insurance, personal gear, and gratuities are generally separate.
When is the best time to go?
The strongest travel window depends on the objective, weather patterns, route conditions, crowds, and your own training runway. We discuss the current local forecast and guide calendar before recommending a window instead of applying a one-size-fits-all date.
How long does each expedition take?
Kilimanjaro has six itinerary options: Machame and Lemosho in 6 or 7 days, plus Marangu in 5 or 6 days. Volcán de Fuego is typically a focused multi-day volcanic trek. Wildlife safaris are tailored around the climbing itinerary, recovery needs, and the parks you want to experience. Compare Kilimanjaro routes →
What is the weather like?
Expect changeable mountain weather: warm sun, cold nights, wind, rain, and rapid shifts are all possible. Safari conditions are different again. Your gear plan and guide briefing are built around the specific terrain and season, then adjusted for current conditions on the ground.
What gear do I need?
Every trip begins with a reliable layering system, weather protection, appropriate footwear, hydration, and a pack that fits. We review the equipment you already own before suggesting purchases, so you arrive with a system rather than an expensive pile of gear. See Gear Help →
How should I train?
Build a durable aerobic base, add sustained uphill hiking or stairs, practice carrying a pack, strengthen legs and core, and leave room for recovery. Your training should prepare you for consecutive days outside, not just a single hard workout.
Is mountain travel dangerous?
There is real risk in any mountain environment: altitude, weather, terrain, fatigue, and changing conditions. Good planning reduces risk through the right route, capable local guides, medical awareness, emergency plans, and the willingness to turn around when needed. No ethical operator guarantees a summit.
Why are your prices often lower than larger operators?
Outland works directly with vetted local guide companies instead of layering large overhead, generic marketing systems, and multiple middlemen on top of the trip. Lower cost never means cutting safety, fair compensation, route time, or preparation support.
Can I add a wildlife safari to Kilimanjaro?
Yes. We can coordinate a wildlife safari after your climb with recovery time and travel buffers built into the plan. Choose the “Kilimanjaro + Wildlife Safari Add-On” option when you contact us, and we will shape the experience around your route and dates.
Do I need travel insurance?
Comprehensive medical and evacuation insurance appropriate to the altitude is required for Kilimanjaro. Other program requirements are confirmed in your written proposal. We strongly recommend cancellation and interruption coverage for every trip; check activity limits and exclusions before buying.
Will I know who is guiding me?
You will know the guide-team plan before departure and will have clear communication with Outland throughout preparation. In-country, the vetted local team leads the expedition with the local knowledge that matters most.
Can I join as a solo traveler?
Often, yes. We can discuss an established departure, a private guide arrangement, or the right way to pair you with other climbers. The best answer depends on your route, dates, and comfort with group travel.
How far ahead should I book?
More lead time gives you better route, permit, flight, training, and guide-calendar options. Reach out as soon as you have a rough travel window; we can tell you what is realistic without pressuring you to commit.
Can you help me choose the right Kilimanjaro route?
Yes. We compare Machame 6-Day, Machame 7-Day, Lemosho 6-Day, Lemosho 7-Day, Marangu 5-Day, and Marangu 6-Day based on your hiking history, preferred pace, available time, and how deliberately you want to acclimatize. Explore the route profiles →
