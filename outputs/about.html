The people behind Outland

Built around
real connection.

Outland Guides was created by two friends who believe the best mountain experiences come from capable local people, honest preparation, and shared respect for the places we travel.

Meet the founders

Caleb & Adam.

Two mountain people, connecting climbers with local professionals who deserve to be found.

Co-founder · Colorado

Caleb Bennett

Caleb calls Colorado home and rarely heads out without his high-adventure Aussie–border collie. After building out and living from his own Sprinter van for a year, he learned that the best routes are rarely the ones that look perfect on paper—they are the ones shaped by genuine people and hard-earned local knowledge.

He is drawn to real experiences, deep connection, and making the world feel a little smaller by bringing the right people together. Outland is a natural extension of that: connecting committed climbers with remarkable local mountain professionals across the globe.

Co-founder · Utah & Colorado

Adam Dimmitt

Adam is from the United States and spends most of his time in Utah and Colorado. A Purdue University graduate and proud owner of a golden retriever named Cooper, he enjoys connecting with people and learning about the experiences that shape them.

In the mountains, he has made countless friends by joining forces, learning from others, and sharing what he knows. That is where the best bonds are made—and it is how he met Caleb, his good friend and co-founder. Their numerous adventures together became the beginning of Outland Guides.

Why we do it

The climb is
never just yours.

There is a moment on a mountain when the group settles into a shared rhythm: boots on rock, breath in thin air, a guide reading the weather, someone quietly offering a hand. That is the part of mountain travel we care about—the work, trust, and people behind the summit photo.

Outland exists to make those connections easier to find. We help climbers arrive prepared and give local mountain teams the visibility, respect, and steady work they have earned through years on their home terrain.

We are here for the long route, the better plan, and the people who make a place feel real. When the right climber meets the right local team, everyone gets more out of the mountain.

What makes us different

A better way
to find your line.

Our mission is simple: make world-class mountain objectives more personal, more transparent, and more connected to the local people who know them best.

We do not optimize an itinerary for the cheapest-looking number. We select the route, pace, and local partner that gives you the strongest chance of a safe, meaningful summit—then help you understand why that is the right call.

TPO & KGA certified

Our Kilimanjaro partnerships follow recognized local standards, including TPO and KGA certification. Credentials matter, but so does how a team treats its climbers, porters, and mountain.

Keep the mountain local

We work directly with local guide companies—not through an anonymous booking marketplace. That keeps expertise, opportunity, and value closer to the communities that make each expedition possible.

Tourism with a human impact

In mountain towns, responsible tourism supports guides, porters, drivers, cooks, lodges, and families. Around Kilimanjaro in particular, tourism is a vital source of local work—one reason we choose our partners carefully.

Summit success over margin

We recommend the better route and the right acclimatization profile for your objective, even when that means a different answer than the fastest or least expensive option.

Free personal consults

We will meet you on Zoom, Teams, or the video platform that works for you. Bring your questions, your goals, and your honest starting point—we will give you an honest path forward.

Real conversations, real proof

We are happy to share verified traveler feedback and walk through the planning process before you commit. You should know who is supporting your expedition and what comes next.
