Privacy policy

Outland Guides ("Outland," "we," "us") coordinates locally guided mountain trips and safaris, with planning support based in Colorado. Contact info@outlandguides.com about this policy or your information.

Information you choose to provide

  • Inquiries: your name, email, optional phone number, selected expedition, travel window, group details, experience, and the questions or notes you send.
  • Route finder: answers remain in the page's memory until you choose to open an inquiry with the summary. You may edit or remove that summary before sending. Completing the finder alone does not create a booking or submit an inquiry.
  • Accounts and bookings: your sign-in email, profile information, requested trips and dates, group size, messages, quotes, confirmations, and payment records.
  • Trip preparation: relevant equipment, dietary, accessibility, emergency-contact, or health information you choose to share so that a trip can be planned. Please do not put passport scans, card numbers, or detailed medical records in a general inquiry. Ask us how to supply sensitive documents when they are needed.

Information collected by the services you use

Our hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, browser and device details, request times, security logs, and referring pages to deliver and protect the website. Hosted payment pages and embedded maps may receive technical information when you use them.

How we use information

We use information to respond personally, assess the suitability and availability of a trip, prepare quotes, coordinate agreed travel services, provide account access, process and reconcile payments, handle cancellations, support travelers, maintain required business records, and prevent fraud or misuse. We use your contact details for relevant inquiry and trip communications. A trip inquiry is not permission for unrelated promotional messaging.

Where data-protection law applies, our processing depends on the purpose: steps you request before a booking, performing the agreed services, legal obligations, legitimate operational and security interests, or consent where required. You can decline optional preparation information; some services may not be possible without the information necessary to deliver them safely.

Who receives information

We do not sell personal information. We share relevant information only as needed to provide the services you request, protect rights and safety, or comply with applicable law.

  • Forms: website inquiries are delivered through Formspree to Outland. Read the Formspree privacy policy.
  • Hosting and email: SiteGround and the email services used for inquiries and sign-in codes process information needed to host the site, maintain accounts, and deliver communications.
  • Payments: Stripe processes payment details through its hosted checkout. Outland receives the payment status, amounts, booking references, and transaction identifiers needed to manage your account. We do not store full card numbers or card security codes on this website. Read Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Local service providers: guides, operators, lodges, transport providers, and relevant park or permit authorities may receive the traveler and itinerary information necessary to arrange and deliver the confirmed services. Health or dietary details are shared only where relevant to preparation, accommodation, or safety. Emergency information may be shared to protect life or health.
  • Your group: a person making a group booking can see and manage the group booking and its payments. Obtain permission before submitting another person's information. We do not make a public traveler directory.
  • Legal and operational needs: professional advisers, authorities, or a successor business may receive necessary records where lawfully required or appropriate, with safeguards relevant to the circumstances.

International trips and information transfers

Outland operates from the United States and arranges services in other countries. Information may be processed in the United States, your destination, and locations used by our providers. Privacy laws can differ. Where required, transfers and disclosures are subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

Booking terms & our role

Outland is your planning and booking coordination point. We help select a suitable option, review preparation needs, coordinate quotes and dates, communicate with local providers, and administer the booking and payments you make through us.

Independent local guides and operators deliver the in-country trip. They are responsible for the on-the-ground services they undertake, their personnel and equipment, local operating compliance, route execution, and operational safety decisions. The guide or local operator has authority to change a day's plan, deny an unsafe optional activity, turn a group around, or require a participant to stop when conditions or readiness warrant it.

That division of roles does not remove Outland's responsibility for its own agreed services or any responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded. It does not guarantee that every loss is solely a guide's responsibility.

When a request becomes a confirmed booking

Submitting a contact form, using the route finder, creating an account, or requesting dates does not reserve permits, cabins, guide capacity, or a departure. We confirm availability and the agreed trip in writing before enabling payment. Your written proposal or confirmation identifies the itinerary, travelers, dates, price, inclusions, exclusions, payment deadline, and any disclosed trip-specific terms. A booking is confirmed only after the required acceptance and payment and our written confirmation. Do not book non-refundable travel based only on a website estimate.

Your written booking agreement and any expressly accepted trip-specific terms control details specific to your booking. These general policies apply where that agreement does not specify otherwise, subject to mandatory consumer rights. Separate operator activity waivers or participation requirements may apply and should be reviewed before participation.

Group bookings and accurate information

The booking organizer must have authority to request services for the selected travelers and share relevant information with their permission. Each participant must understand the itinerary and preparation requirements. Changes to traveler count, dates, accommodation, or add-ons can change the price and require a revised confirmation. You are responsible for accurate contact and traveler information and for keeping access to your sign-in email secure.

Prices, deposits & balances

Prices are in US dollars unless the written proposal states otherwise. Kilimanjaro prices depend on the route, mountain days, and group size. Standard online Kilimanjaro and safari quotes require at least two travelers; solo arrangements need a personal quote. Safari packages may have a lower combined rate when agreed with a Kilimanjaro booking. Website estimates are subject to availability and written confirmation; we will disclose and obtain agreement to changes before taking payment.

Fuego's shared five-person cabin is $200 per person for the one-night program, for one to five travelers in a single-cabin booking. A private two-person cabin is $499 total for the cabin and covers exactly two travelers. Multiple-cabin arrangements require confirmation; the overall Fuego group maximum is 15. Optional night treks, porters, rentals, or extra services are included only when the written proposal lists them.

  • Deposit: 50% of the agreed program price is normally due to reserve a confirmed place.
  • Balance: the standard deadline is 60 days before departure. For a booking made within 60 days, we specify the closer-in payment deadline before you pay; full payment may be required sooner. The final date is stated in your written confirmation, and all required fees must be paid before departure.
  • Registration fee: any non-refundable registration fee included in a deposit must be itemized and disclosed before payment. If no separate registration fee was disclosed and agreed, we do not deduct an undisclosed fee from a refund.
  • Group payments: a payment covers the travelers and services shown in that booking. A group's per-person rate may change if its size changes; a revised amount must be confirmed.
  • Checkout: Stripe handles the payment method. Card-issuer currency-conversion or international charges, if any, are outside Outland's quoted price. Payment failures do not create a reservation.

Unless expressly included, plan separately for international flights, visas and passports, insurance, personal gear, gratuities, independent travel, upgrades, and optional services. Check the route-specific equipment list for what is provided, personal, or rented.

Cancellations & refunds

Trip preparation involves advance permits and local operating commitments. We strongly recommend trip cancellation and interruption insurance. A booking made close to departure can fall immediately inside a non-refundable period; review these terms before paying.

For the general policy below, days are counted before the confirmed program start date. Send cancellation or refund requests by email to info@outlandguides.com; the date we receive the email determines the applicable period. Include your booking reference and traveler names.

If Outland cancels or reschedules before the trip

If Outland cancels or reschedules your program before it begins, fees paid to Outland for that program are refundable or transferable, at your choice. This does not automatically reimburse independently purchased flights, visas, insurance, or other third-party expenses. Please contact us so the refund or transfer can be documented.

Changes during the trip

Weather, volcanic activity, park decisions, illness, transport disruption, or safety judgments can change or shorten a trip. A summit, eruption, wildlife sighting, or completion of an optional trek is never guaranteed. Refunds for changes after the program begins depend on the accepted agreement, services provided, recoverable provider funds, and applicable law. There is no automatic refund solely because an objective was not reached; this does not remove any legal right to a refund.

Approved refunds are ordinarily returned through the original payment method. Processing and bank posting times vary. We will explain the approved calculation and status rather than promise a bank-processing date we do not control.

Risk, readiness & responsibility

Mountain travel, active-volcano trekking, safaris, road transfers, and remote travel involve risks including altitude illness, injury, changing weather, cold, falls, wildlife, volcanic hazards, vehicle incidents, delays, and limited access to medical care. Serious injury or death is possible. No route, guide credential, safety equipment, or planning process eliminates these risks.

Your preparation

  • Provide accurate experience and preparation information, train for the agreed itinerary, and bring or arrange all required personal equipment.
  • Consult a qualified clinician about fitness for altitude or remote travel, medications, vaccinations, and relevant health conditions. Website content and the route finder are planning aids, not medical advice or clearance.
  • Arrange required passports, visas, entry documents, and insurance. Comprehensive medical and evacuation coverage appropriate to the altitude and activities is required for Kilimanjaro. Other program requirements are confirmed in writing; cancellation and interruption coverage are strongly recommended for all programs.
  • Follow local laws, park rules, and guide instructions. Report symptoms and concerns promptly. A guide's turnaround or closure decision takes priority over a planned summit or optional activity.
  • Avoid behavior that puts the group, crew, wildlife, or community at risk. Unsafe conduct or inability to meet participation requirements can lead to removal from an activity, subject to the booking agreement and applicable law.
